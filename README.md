## New in 5.1
- Calories: one thick ring with a roomy centre plus thick flat macro bars. The old concentric rings are still
  available (Settings > Home screen, or press and hold the greeting on Home) and now fit the centre number.
- Home: choose how many and which nutrients are highlighted, reorder or hide sections.
- Log: entries first, favourites under them (stored on this phone only, tap = now), AI helper at the bottom.

## Updating
Same signing key + rising version code: install the new APK over the old one and Android updates it in place.
